# Your balance, always visible

Your remaining DeepSeek credits appear in the status bar and refresh
automatically (every 5 minutes by default — adjustable in settings).

- The item turns **yellow** when the balance drops below a warning threshold
- It turns **red** when credits are critically low or depleted
- The tooltip shows your estimated daily spend and how long your credits will last
- Click it any time to refresh, switch USD/CNY, top up, or open the dashboard
